---
description: Write cards inside notes; review them from the Study panel.
slug: flashcards
---

```md
Question:: What does a mitochondrion produce?
Answer:: ATP.
```

`Q::`/`A::` and `front :: back` work too. Review with `Space` to reveal, `1` Again, `2` Good, `3` Easy. Neoma keeps your last rating per card; it doesn't schedule by interval.

An exam page (`type: exam`, `exam-date:`) counts down in the Study panel and shows on the calendar.
