| Vault | Lives in |
| --- | --- |
| **Local folder** | a folder you choose. Any editor, backup or sync tool can use it. |
| **Inside Neoma** | the browser or app's storage, on this device only. Export it regularly. |

Dot-folders like `.git` and `.obsidian` are skipped. Settings, flashcard ratings and the trash are kept by the app, not in the vault. Neoma doesn't encrypt notes; use your device's disk encryption.
