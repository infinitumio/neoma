---
description: Checkboxes with a date, a priority or #task are gathered into Tasks.
slug: tasks
---

```md
## Tasks
- [ ] Email supervisor
- [ ] Submit ethics form 📅 2026-10-12 ⏫
```

Priority: `⏫` high, `🔼` medium, `🔽` low. Course: `#course/physics`. A checkbox under a **Tasks** or **To-do** heading counts too; other checklists stay plain.

The panel groups **Today**, **Upcoming** and **Done**. Ticking one ticks it in its note. `🔁` repeats are read but don't create the next task yet.
