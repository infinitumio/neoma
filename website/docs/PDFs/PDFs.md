---
description: Read papers in Neoma's own offline PDF viewer, and link straight to a page.
slug: pdfs
---

Drag a PDF into your vault, or add it from the attachment picker. Click it to open the viewer.

- **Select and copy text.** Find in document, from the toolbar, highlights every match.
- **Thumbnails, zoom, fit width or page, rotate, fullscreen, print.**
- **Link to a page:** `[[paper.pdf#page=12]]` opens the PDF at page 12.
- **Split view** puts the PDF on the left and a companion note on the right, for paraphrasing as you read. The note is filed under the PDF.
- `![[paper.pdf]]` in a note shows a preview card of the first page.

The viewer is bundled with Neoma, so PDFs open offline and are never uploaded. Recent PDFs appear on the Study dashboard. See [[Images and files]] for how attachments are stored.
