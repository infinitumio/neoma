description: Folders, pages inside pages, drag to move. Links follow.
slug: folders
---

```text
Machine Learning/
├── Machine Learning.md   ← the page
├── Regression.md         ← its child pages
└── Neural Networks.md
```

- **Child page**: right-click → **New subpage**, `/subpage`, or drag one page onto another.
- **Move**: drag onto a folder, or right-click → **Move…**. Links update after a preview.
- **Colour**, **Pin** and sort (name, created, modified) are in the right-click menu and the Files toolbar.
- **Deleted** pages go to **Recently deleted**. See [[Back up and move notes]].
