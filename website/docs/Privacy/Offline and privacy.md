---
description: Notes leave your device only when you export or publish them.
slug: privacy
---

No account, no analytics, no AI. The web app works offline after the first visit.

Three things can touch the network, each one by your choice:

1. **Check for updates** in the desktop app.
2. **Remote images and videos**, if you turn on **Load remote content**.
3. **Links you click.**

The app's Content Security Policy blocks every other connection, and a test fails the build if it tries one. Read the [policy](https://neomadev.app/#open-source) and the [network audit](https://neomadev.app/writing/network-audit.html).
