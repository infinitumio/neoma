Keep typing to filter: `/ta` finds **Table**. `↑` `↓` to move, `Enter` to insert, `Esc` to close, `Tab` to jump between placeholders.

There are about a hundred blocks: text, lists, [[Callouts]], [[Math]], research and study templates, [[Images and files|media]], tasks and dates. Pin favourites with ★. Every item is also in `Mod+K`.
