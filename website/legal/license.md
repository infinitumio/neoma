### Why the difference?

Copyleft licenses like the AGPL cannot lawfully be distributed through the App
Store. The AGPL forbids imposing additional restrictions on the people who receive
the software, and Apple's terms impose several — notably limits on how many devices
a copy may be installed on. This is not a technicality: VLC was removed from the
App Store in 2011 for exactly this reason.

Rather than drop the open-source edition, Neoma is dual-licensed. Because Neoma has
a single author, that author can offer the same work under the AGPL to everyone and
under proprietary terms to the app stores. Both editions are genuine — the open one
isn't a crippled demo, and the store one isn't a different program.

Neoma collects, transmits or has access to them. See the
