## Short announcement

Neoma brings research notes, linked ideas and study cards together in a local-first
notebook. Write in Markdown, connect pages with wiki-links, and review flashcards
offline. No account is required.

Try the web app: https://neomadev.app/app
Explore the open-source web edition: https://github.com/infinitumio/neoma

## Developer introduction

I'm an independent developer based in Australia, building Neoma for research and
study. It combines Markdown editing, linked notes, a graph, daily notes, a calendar
and flashcards. Your notes stay on your device, with ZIP export for backups and
manual transfers between devices.

The web edition is AGPL-licensed and self-hostable. Native apps are packaged and
licensed separately. I'd welcome feedback on the editor, navigation and study tools.
