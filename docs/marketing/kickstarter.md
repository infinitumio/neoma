## The pitch (main story body)

### Your notes should belong to you

Most note apps quietly sync your private thinking to someone else's servers. You
rent access to your own ideas, and if the company pivots or shuts down, your
knowledge goes with it.

Neoma is the opposite. It is a research notebook that keeps every note as a plain
Markdown file on your own device. No account. No telemetry. No cloud required. It
boots and works with your Wi-Fi switched off, and your notes are just files you
already own.

**And you can try it right now, in your browser, with nothing to install:**
https://neomadev.app/app

### What Neoma already does (today, for free)

- A calm, keyboard-driven Markdown editor with a slash menu for anything: headings, callouts, tables, math, flashcards.
- Wiki-links, backlinks, and a live graph view so you can see the shape of your thinking.
- A built-in calendar, daily notes, and task lists.
- Spaced-repetition flashcards for studying, fully offline.
- Native apps for macOS and iOS, plus Windows, Linux, and the web.
- Open source under the AGPL. Audit every line.

This is not a promise or a mockup. It is shipping software you can use today.

### What this Kickstarter unlocks: Neoma Pro

Neoma the app is free and open source, forever. What costs money to run, and what
I am raising funds to build, is the optional hosted layer:

- **Encrypted cloud sync** so your vault stays in step across all your devices, end-to-end encrypted so even we cannot read it.
- **One-click publishing** so any note or folder becomes a beautiful website at your own address.
- **Team and shared vaults** for people who work together.

Back this campaign and you pre-order Neoma Pro at a founder price, before it
exists. Your support literally pays for the servers and the development time to
build it.

### Why I need your help

I am a solo developer based in Australia. I have built Neoma in the open because
I believe your knowledge should be rooted locally, not locked in a subscription
on a server on the other side of the world. To keep going, and to build the
hosted features people are asking for, I need this to be sustainable. Backing
this campaign, or sponsoring the project, is what makes that possible.

## Risks and challenges (Kickstarter requires this - be honest, it builds trust)

I have been building Neoma in public and the app is already released, so the core
risk of "will it ever exist" does not apply. The remaining risks:

- **Scope:** hosted sync with end-to-end encryption is genuinely hard to do right. I would rather ship it a few weeks late than ship it insecure. I will post regular, honest development updates.
- **Solo developer:** I am one person. If I am ill or life happens, updates may slow. Because Neoma is fully open source and your notes are plain files on your device, you are never locked in or left stranded even in the worst case.
- **Pricing:** Pro will be priced cheaply, roughly to cover hosting. Backers always keep the founder pricing promised here regardless of future changes.

## FAQ

**Is the app really free?**
Yes. Every platform, no paid tier for the app itself. Neoma is open source under
the AGPL. The Kickstarter funds the optional hosted Pro services.

**What if the project stops?**
Your notes are plain Markdown files you already control, and the code is public
and can be self-hosted or forked. You are never locked in.

**Do I need Pro to use Neoma?**
No. The full local-first app is free forever. Pro only adds hosted convenience
(sync, publishing) for people who want it.

**When will Pro ship?**
The goal is within 3 months of a funded campaign. Backers get access first.

**Can I self-host the sync server?**
That is the plan. The sync layer will be documented so you can run your own.
